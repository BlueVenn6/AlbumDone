# AlbumDone Privacy Policy — Mobile and Windows desktop

English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

AlbumDone is developed by Link Vector Pty Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use AlbumDone and contact us about it.

Photos and local processing

AlbumDone helps you review photos, find duplicates or similar images, and create year-in-review images. These core functions run on your device. We do not receive your photo library or upload your photos to our servers for analysis. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

With your permission, the app reads photos and associated information needed for these functions, such as asset identifiers, dates, filenames, dimensions and available file-size information. It may store thumbnails, image signatures used for comparison, review choices, progress and preferences locally. These records are not sent to us as part of normal app use. A similarity suggestion is not a guarantee that two photos are interchangeable; you decide what to keep or delete.

Permissions, saving and deletion

On iOS, you can grant access to selected photos or your full library, refuse access, or change access in Settings. The app can only work with the photos available to it. Saving generated images requires the relevant system permission. Deletion follows your choices and the operating system's authorization and confirmation process. Once submitted to the system, a deletion may no longer be cancellable in the app. Recovery, if available, follows the operating system's rules. Uninstalling AlbumDone does not itself delete your original photos or images already saved to your library.

On Android, you can allow or refuse photo-library access and change it in the system settings; supported Android versions also allow access to selected photos. The app can only process accessible photos. The Android app disables automatic system backup of its local app records; do not rely on reinstalling it to restore review progress. Google Play handles Google Play purchases; we do not receive your payment-card details.

iOS: Photos will be removed from your library. They can usually be recovered from Recently Deleted for 30 days. With iCloud Photos, deletion also syncs to your other devices.

Android: Photos will be deleted from the device library, not moved to a recycle bin. This cannot be undone in this app.

Apple services, purchases and external pages

If you use iCloud Photos, the operating system may download originals for viewing or processing, and may sync library changes and saved images. Changes to your iCloud photo library, including deletions, may also appear on other devices using the same library. AlbumDone does not provide its own cloud backup service. Whether locally stored preferences and review progress are included in iCloud Backup depends on where and how those records are stored. They may not be restored after reinstalling the app or moving to another device; do not rely on iCloud Backup to recover them. These services are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. Apple also handles App Store payments; we do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may make diagnostic reports available to developers according to your device settings and Apple's policies; the app does not automatically send us its own analytics reports.

Opening our product, privacy or support pages uses your browser and an internet connection. These websites operate separately from on-device photo processing. When you visit them, the website and its service providers may process your IP address, browser information, technical logs and any information you submit through a support form. Cookies may be used according to the website configuration and your choices. Opening a link does not itself send your photo library to the website.

Our website is hosted by Hostinger. Its support form asks for a name, email address and message; a last name is optional. Sending the form submits those details through the website service. The site may use necessary cookies and, with your consent, Hostinger's optional platform analytics, including Amplitude. These website services do not read your photo library through the app.

Support, retention and your choices

Where EU/EEA data protection law applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and keeping the website secure for support messages and essential site logs. Optional website analytics rely on consent where required; records we must retain by law are handled to meet legal obligations. You may withdraw consent and exercise applicable data rights by emailing us. For a privacy complaint, please contact us first; we will investigate and respond. You may also complain to your relevant EU/EEA data protection authority or, where Australian privacy law applies, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) after giving us a reasonable opportunity to respond. Website and email providers may process your contact and site data outside your country, including in Australia; contact us for details of the destinations and applicable transfer safeguards.

If you email us or submit a support request, we receive the contact details and content you provide, which may include attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and maintain necessary support records. Please avoid sending private photos or other unnecessary personal information. Our email, hosting or support providers may process these communications in countries other than yours. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising; we may disclose it where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights.

Local app records remain on your device until removed by the app, by you or by the operating system. Generated caches may be cleared automatically. We retain support records only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or raise a privacy concern. We cannot remotely access or delete your local photo library.

The app is not designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us. We may update this policy and will publish the revised text and date on this page.
